Port & Logistics Glossary

A quick guide to common terminology used around ports, shipping and logistics.

AIS Automatic Identification System used to exchange vessel identification and position information.
Berth A designated location where a vessel is moored for loading, unloading or service.
Drayage Short-distance transportation of freight or containers, often between a port and warehouse or rail terminal.
TEU Twenty-foot equivalent unit, a standard measurement used for containerized cargo.
Demurrage Charges that may apply when cargo or containers remain at a terminal beyond allowed free time.
Detention Charges that may apply when carrier equipment is retained outside the terminal beyond permitted free time.
Intermodal Transportation using more than one mode such as vessel, rail and truck.
Ro-Ro Roll-on/roll-off vessel designed for wheeled cargo such as vehicles and machinery.
MMSI Maritime Mobile Service Identity number used to identify vessels and maritime radio stations.
