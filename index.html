Track Vessels Using an AIS Provider

NOT FOR NAVIGATION

AIS data may be delayed, incomplete, inaccurate or unavailable. Never use this website or consumer AIS information for navigation, collision avoidance, piloting, docking or safety-of-life decisions.

Why isn't there a fake vessel roster?

Vessel names, berth assignments and arrival times change frequently. USPPO.com intentionally does not display hard-coded information as though it were current.

About The Platform

What is USPPO.com?

USPPO.com is an independently operated maritime and logistics information website focused on Sparrows Point, Maryland and the surrounding Baltimore logistics region.

The name references the UN/LOCODE used for Sparrows Point. Use of a location code does not indicate sponsorship, approval, ownership, endorsement or affiliation with the United Nations or any governmental, port, terminal or maritime authority.
